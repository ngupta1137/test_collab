RX EDUCATION SCRIPT - MAIL ORDER   (SYNTHETIC - FOR PROTOTYPE ONLY)
Doc ID: KB-PHARM-0142 | Owner: Pharmacy Ops Knowledge Team | Last edited: 03/14/2026 | Effective: 01/15/2026

[OPENING]
Verbatim (Legal): "Thank you for calling. This call is on a recorded line and may be monitored for quality and training purposes. My name is [Advocate Name]. May I have your full name and date of birth?"
Tip: smile, members can hear it!! Don't rush the verbatim.
Verify identity: full name + DOB + member ID OR address on file (see ID&V article 7731).

[EDUCATION]
Explain mail order benefits: meds delivered to home, 90-day supply available for most maintenance medications, possible savings vs. retail. Do NOT quote $ unless the pricing tool shows member-specific cost.

**Pricing disclaimer**
Verbatim (Legal): "The prices I share today are estimates based on your current plan and may change. Your final cost will be confirmed when your prescription is processed."

Delivery: typically arrives within 7 to 10 business days after the prescription is received from the doctor.
Shipping: standard shipping at no extra cost. [Reviewed 06/2025, next review due 06/30/2026]

[MAIL ORDER SETUP]  (aka "MO setup")
1. Confirm shipping address
2. Confirm payment method on file
3. Ask permission to contact the prescriber
4. Submit request in pharmacy system; read back the confirmation number

[OBJECTIONS]
"I like my local pharmacy" -> acknowledge; member can use both; no pressure, ever.

[CLOSING]
Verbatim (Legal): "Your decision today has no impact on your plan membership. Is there anything else I can help you with today?"
NOTE: old closing ("...won't affect your coverage") RETIRED Jan 2026 - do not use!!
