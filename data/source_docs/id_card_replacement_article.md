ID CARD REPLACEMENT   (SYNTHETIC - FOR PROTOTYPE ONLY)
Doc ID: KB-INS-0057 | Owner: Member Services Knowledge Team | Last edited: 02/10/2026 | Effective: 02/10/2026

Members can request a replacement member ID card through the member portal, the mobile app, or by calling an advocate.
A new card arrives within 7 to 10 days. A temporary card can be printed right away from the member portal.

Changing a primary care provider (PCP):
1. Confirm the new PCP is in network
2. Update the PCP in the member record
3. Tell the member the change is effective the first day of next month
